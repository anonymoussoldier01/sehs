# Victorian Selective Entry High Schools — exam format (verified)

Audience: Year 8 students sitting Year 9 selective entry in Victoria. Authoritative sources below; anything else is marked unverified in the UI.

## Verified (ACER official + Victorian Department of Education)
- Test provider: ACER administers the centralised selection process on behalf of the Department of Education.
  - https://selectiveentry.acer.org/vic
- Entry point: second year of secondary schooling (Year 8) sitting for Year 9 entry; ~1,000 places across four schools: Melbourne High, Mac.Robertson Girls' High, Nossal High, Suzanne Cory High.
  - https://www.vic.gov.au/selective-entry-high-schools
- Three timed blocks (paper-based, answer booklets, machine-scanned MCQ shading):
  1. Mathematics + Quantitative Reasoning — 60 minutes.
  2. Reading + Verbal Reasoning — 55 minutes.
  3. Writing — 40 minutes, two tasks.
- Exam tasks total 2 hours 35 minutes; full administration ~4 hours including registration and breaks (20-min break + 5-min short break).
  - https://selectiveentry.acer.org/vic/prepare and https://selectiveentry.acer.org/vic/exam-day
- Curriculum cap: required knowledge does not exceed the Year 8 curriculum. Calculators not permitted (site copy; consistent with paper-booklet format — calculator rule itself is site guidance, treat as verified-practice rather than ACER-quoted).
- Writing: two tasks in 40 minutes (commonly one persuasive + one creative/narrative in practice; ACER describes "two tasks" and "precise language with ideas organised coherently" — genre split marked **unverified** where stated).

## Unverified (do not present as official)
- Per-block question counts (third-party sites claim e.g. ~50–60 per section; ACER does not publish counts). Site mocks pace against minutes, not assumed counts.
- A conflicting third-party "four components + separate timings" description (selectivetrial, 2026-05-18) contradicts ACER's 3-block structure — rejected in favour of ACER official.
- Scoring/cut-offs and offer rank modelling — never shown; no result predictions (non-goal).

## Section types prioritised (from verified format)
Single-answer multiple choice (all reasoning blocks), reading comprehension (passage + linked questions), quantitative/mathematical problem solving (numeric response with tolerance where practised off-paper), writing prompts (planning guidance + transparent criteria; feedback labelled as practice, not official marking).
